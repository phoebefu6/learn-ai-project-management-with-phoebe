Learn AI + Project Management with Phoebe

Two tracks for people who actually have to deliver

A leader track for heads of delivery and PMO leads - where AI helps, where it quietly wrecks trust, and how to govern it without slowing anything down. And a PM track that builds the whole delivery toolkit with Claude sitting next to you: charter, milestones, dependencies, risk register, status report, escalations, and a weekly pack that runs itself. One running programme carries all sixteen sessions. Around half of project professionals say AI already touches their work, while only about a fifth have real hands-on experience. This closes that gap - without ever letting the model commit a date.

The three rails this whole course runs on: context beats prompting - a weak draft means a missing document, not a weak prompt. The dates come from the team - AI drafts the structure of a plan, never the commitments. And a named human signs it - every artifact that leaves the workspace has one accountable person who read it. Northwind is a fictional retailer and every number in it is invented.
